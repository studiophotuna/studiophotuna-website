One App, From Setup to Printed Strip

Studio Photuna covers both halves of a photo booth business: the experience your guests touch, and the workspace you run it from. Everything below is part of the same Windows application — there are no paid add-on modules.

Explore Demo

Guest Experience

A single timed session from the welcome screen to the printed strip. The guest taps to start, picks a layout, gets photographed, reviews and retakes, applies a tone and frame, then collects both a print and a QR gallery.

Which steps appear is up to you — an attended event booth and a self-service one run the same engine with a different flow.

Step through it

Operator Dashboard

Your side of the booth. Create an event workspace per client, connect and test the camera and printer, watch sessions as they happen, and pull the numbers afterwards.

  • Events, each with their own templates, branding and settings
  • Camera, printing, storage and system settings in one place
  • Remote Booth — check on a running booth, or stop it, from elsewhere
  • Reports on sessions, prints and takings per event
See the dashboard

Templates & Branding

Design the strip your guests keep. The template editor works in 4x6, 6x4, 2x6 and 6x2 with a drag-and-drop photo placeholder, slider controls, dark mode and a fullscreen editing mode for large screens.

Layouts

Four strip formats, each with its own slot arrangement and aspect ratio locked by default so nothing distorts.

Branding

Logos, frames and overlay borders per event, plus a branded welcome screen so the booth looks like the client's.

Frames & effects

Tone presets and frames the guest picks at the booth, previewed live against their own photos.

Payments

Charge at the booth for a self-service setup, or skip payment entirely for an event you have already been booked for.

Support for online and cash payment workflows depends on your configured payment gateway and hardware setup. Not every payment method works with every setup, so check the Help Center for what your configuration supports before an event.

QR Gallery & Printing

Guests walk away with both. The strip prints on your dye-sublimation printer while a QR code on screen gives them full-resolution photos on their phone — no app to install.

  • Printing with control over margins, orientation and copies per session
  • A gallery generated per session, reachable by QR scan
  • Galleries kept for 6 months on monthly, 12 months on yearly

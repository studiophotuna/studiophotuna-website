1 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Studio Photuna ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal data when you use our Korean-style photobooth business software platform, including our website, desktop application, event services, and any related tools or features (collectively, the "Service").

This Policy applies to all users of the Service, including registered photobooth operators, their staff, event guests whose photos are captured, and general website visitors. By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy is drafted in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (the Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU Regulation 2016/679), and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) as retained and amended under the UK Data Protection Act 2018, in each case where applicable to you.

2 Our Role as Data Controller and Processor

Studio Photuna plays different legal roles depending on what data is involved. Understanding this distinction matters because it determines who you should contact first, and who is legally responsible for what.

  • We are the Data Controller for: your operator account information (name, email address, billing details, and usage data), general website visitor data, and any support enquiries you send us. We decide why and how this data is processed, and this Privacy Policy governs it directly.
  • We are a Data Processor for: guest photographs and event session data captured through the photo booth. This data is processed strictly on the instructions of the booth operator who ran the event, under the Data Processing Agreement set out in our Operator Agreement.
  • Booth operators are the Data Controllers for their guests' photos and event data. If you are a business running a Studio Photuna–powered photo booth at your event, you decide why photos are taken and how they are used, and you are legally responsible for that decision. We simply process the data on your behalf and under your instructions.

In practice, this means booth guests should generally raise questions about a specific event or photo session with the operator who ran that event first, though we also provide a direct route to us — see Section 10 and Section 11 below.

4 What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data depending on how you interact with the Service:

Providing a valid payment method is required to access paid subscription tiers. Guest photo capture, by contrast, is entirely voluntary: guests who prefer not to be photographed may decline on the booth consent screen and will not be photographed or otherwise processed.

6 Legal Bases for Processing

Under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012):

  • Consent: Where you have given clear and voluntary consent for the processing of your personal data for one or more specific purposes (Section 12(a), RA 10173).
  • Fulfillment of Contract: Where processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party, or in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Section 12(b), RA 10173).
  • Legitimate Interest: Where processing is necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party, provided that such interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights and freedoms (Section 12(f), RA 10173).
  • Legal Obligation: Where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject, including BIR tax regulations and NPC compliance requirements (Section 12(c), RA 10173).

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR (where applicable):

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Where you have given explicit consent to the processing of your personal data.
  • Performance of a Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): Where processing is necessary for the performance of our service agreement with you.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as fraud prevention and service improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): Where processing is required to comply with EU, UK, or Member State law.

Legal Basis for Guest Photographs

Guest photographs captured at the booth are treated separately, on a higher legal basis than general account or website data:

  • Under GDPR and UK GDPR: Explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a)).
  • Under RA 10173: Consent (Section 12(a)).

This consent is collected through the consent screen shown on the photo booth device immediately before each session begins — guests must tick a checkbox and select "I Agree — Continue" before any photo is taken. Because a photograph of a person's face can be treated as biometric-adjacent personal data, we apply the higher Article 9 consent standard to this processing as a matter of caution. Processing does not involve facial recognition or automated identification.

10 Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Be Informed: You have the right to be informed of the collection and processing of your personal data, including the purpose, scope, and method of processing.
  • Right to Access: You have the right to obtain a copy of your personal data that we hold, along with information about how it has been used or disclosed.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data, including processing for direct marketing, automated processing, or profiling.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You have the right to request the suspension, withdrawal, blocking, removal, or destruction of your personal data from our systems.
  • Right to Damages: You have the right to be indemnified for any damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to obtain your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to have it transmitted to another data controller where technically feasible.
  • Right to File a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint before the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your data privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at dpo@studiophotuna.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days, or as required by applicable law.

  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): The right to request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): The right to request deletion of your personal data where there is no compelling reason for its continued processing.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18): The right to request that we limit the processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL protocols and at rest within our database systems.

  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic vulnerability assessments and security reviews of our infrastructure, codebase, and third-party integrations.
  • Incident Response: We maintain a data breach response plan and will notify affected data subjects and the NPC within the timeframes required by RA 10173 and the GDPR in the event of a personal data breach.

While we strive to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining industry-standard protections.

Quick Self-Service

  • Camera not detected? Check that your camera is switched to manual focus and its USB mode is set to PC Control.
  • Printer margins offset? Open Settings → Printing and click "Auto-Align Border".
  • QR link not loading for guests? Check the booth's Wi-Fi connection — QR delivery needs an active internet connection to upload.

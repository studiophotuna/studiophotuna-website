Use the Equipment That Fits Your Setup

Studio Photuna is software, not a kiosk. It runs on a standard Windows PC and drives the camera and printer you choose, so you are not locked into buying a specific booth.

Computer

Operating systemWindows 10 or Windows 11
Form factorAny PC or laptop. A laptop on a tripod is enough to start; a kiosk enclosure is optional.
ConnectivityUSB ports for the camera and printer. The app scans connected ports and auto-detects supported devices.
InternetCapture, template rendering and printing work offline. QR cloud sharing, session syncing and remote dashboard access need a connection at the venue.

Cameras

Studio Photuna controls the camera over USB, giving full-resolution photos, live view and exposure control from inside the app.

Webcams

Any webcam works as a fallback. It is the simplest way to learn the software before committing to a camera, and a reasonable backup to keep in the kit.

Support is per-model rather than per-brand and the tested list grows over time. If your exact body is not listed, ask via the Help Center before buying.

Printers

Printing works with standard dye-sublimation photo printers — the type used across the photo booth industry. Inside the app you control margins, orientation, cut borders and the number of copies per session.

Templates print at 4x6, 6x4, 2x6 and 6x2, so match your printer's media to the strip formats you intend to sell.

Payment setup

If you are running a self-service booth that takes money at the point of use, the payment side depends on two things you choose: the payment gateway you configure, and the hardware you pair with it.

There is support for online and cash payment workflows, but not every payment method works with every setup. Confirm your intended combination through the Help Center before buying payment hardware.

Setup

  1. 1Download and sign in. Install the Windows app and activate the 14-day trial from inside it.
  2. 2Connect the camera. Plug it in over USB and pick it in Settings → Camera, then set resolution and capture behaviour.
  3. 3Connect the printer. Configure margins, orientation and copies per session in Settings → Printing.
  4. 4Build a template and an event. Design the strip, apply your branding, then create the event workspace the booth will run.

One Platform. Different Ways to Run Your Booth.

Studio Photuna is not built around a single style of booth. The guest flow is configurable, so the same software runs an attended wedding booth, an unattended paid one in a mall, and everything in between.

Event Photo Booth

Weddings, parties, corporate events — anywhere you or your staff are on site running the booth.

  • One event workspace per client, with its own templates and branding
  • Skip the payment step entirely when the event is already paid for
  • Prints and a QR gallery for every guest

Photo Booth Vendo

A booth that earns without someone standing beside it. The guest chooses a package, pays, shoots and collects — the booth resets itself for the next person.

  • Package selection and payment built into the guest flow
  • Auto-reset between sessions, with kiosk recovery after a restart
  • Remote Booth to check on or stop a booth you are not standing next to

Support for online and cash payment workflows depends on your configured payment gateway and hardware setup.

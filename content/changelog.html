v0.4.8

Receipts & Reliable Payments

September 12, 2026

Every payment now has a receipt you can print, and a payment finishes on its own whether or not the checkout window is still open.

New

  • • Billing history. Every payment on your account, from the app and from studiophotuna.com alike, under Billing and on your Account page. Open one for a receipt carrying the plan, the period it covers, how you paid and the reference — save it as a PDF or print it. Amounts show what was actually charged, so a discounted payment reads as what you really paid.
  • • PayPal payments complete on our side. A payment goes through whether the app is open, closed, or on another machine, and you land on a result screen that says what happened — paid, cancelled, declined or under review — instead of the home page.
  • • PayMongo payments are confirmed even with the window closed, so paying by QR on your phone after closing checkout still activates your plan.
  • • The checkout dialog re-checks when you return to the app, offers an “I've paid — check now” button, and takes one last look as you close it.

Fixed

  • • A payment could be approved and then reversed because nothing finalised it once the checkout window closed.
  • • Payment problems showed a technical message instead of the reason.
  • • Print and Save as PDF on a receipt produced a blank page.
  • • Booth timers froze while another window sat in front of the app.
  • • A plan is applied once per payment, and a failed activation is reported rather than showing a plan that was never saved.
v0.4.7

Device Seats & Per-Device Monitoring

September 11, 2026

Each plan now covers a set number of booth devices, and you can see, name and manage every device on your account.

New

  • • Device seats per plan: Free 1, Trial 2, Monthly 3, Yearly 5. Each computer running the app uses one seat. Phones and browsers used for Remote Booth never do.
  • • Every device, individually. Account Center → Devices lists each one with a name you can change (it starts as the Windows computer name), its type, the app version it runs, when it was last used, and its seat number, so you can tell your booths apart and spot one that hasn't updated.
  • • Your count wherever you look: in the Account Center header, as a meter on the Billing page next to Events and Templates, and on your studiophotuna.com Account page.
  • • Free a seat on the spot. If every seat is taken, a new device shows your devices with a Release button, so you can make room without needing another computer. Devices unused for 90 days are released automatically.

Your devices stay working

  • • A device already on your account is never locked out, even if you're over your plan's number. The limit only applies to adding a new one.
  • • A booth that's offline when it opens starts normally. A device is never blocked because the check couldn't be made.

Fixed

  • • A booth in daily use could look inactive and be cleared after 90 days, because its last-used time was only recorded the first time it signed in. It now updates every time the app opens.
  • • Releasing a device could say it had been released even when the release failed.
  • • Booth PCs set up from the same disk image are now counted as separate devices instead of being merged into one.
v0.4.6

PayPal Checkout & Device Management

September 11, 2026

You can now pay for your plan with PayPal as well as PayMongo, and see every machine signed into your account.

New

  • • Choose how you pay for your plan. Subscription checkout now offers PayMongo (GCash, Maya, GrabPay, cards) or PayPal (PayPal balance, cards). The same choice is available on studiophotuna.com.
  • • Devices tab in Account Center. Every machine signed into your account, what it is, when it was last used, and which one you are on. Release a booth PC you have sold or reformatted, and use Sign out everywhere to end every session at once if a machine goes missing.
  • • Payment gateway setup rebuilt. Connecting a gateway is now three steps — choose a provider, connect your account, switch it on for an event — each ticking off as you finish it, so it is clear what is left before a guest can be charged. A summary panel shows your gateway, whether it is live or in test mode, your currency, and how many events are actually charging.
  • • Help Center rewritten. Troubleshooting for the problems that stop a booth soonest, each leading with the fix; a pre-event checklist; and what each Remote Booth button actually does.
  • • Camera settings show what your camera can really do. Resolutions beyond your camera's maximum are greyed out, and the measured output size is shown with a flag when the camera returns something smaller than requested.

Security

  • • Closed a gap in subscription activation where a payment made for one plan could be used to claim a more expensive one. The plan and the buyer are now read back from the payment itself rather than taken on trust.

Fixed

  • • Yearly subscribers who signed up through the website were getting 6 months of gallery retention instead of 12, and their plan name did not show in Billing. Both fixed.
  • • Help and setup text pointed at sections that had since been renamed.
  • • Dark mode now covers the account tab bar, the security rows and the new panels, which previously had no dark styling at all.

A pass over where every setting lives and how it looks, plus gallery access moving to every paid plan.

v0.4.1

Event Isolation, Full Screen & Security Hardening

September 7, 2026

Events are properly isolated from one another: a new event no longer inherits the last one's branding, settings or design, and Templates and Frames now show what is actually applied to the event you have open. The app also opens full screen, and a storage-cleanup permission gap has been closed.

New

  • • Opens full screen — Fills the display on launch; F11 toggles.
  • • Copy templates and frames to a new event — An optional toggle when creating an event, listing each source event with its template and frame counts. Branding and settings always start fresh, and the source event is never modified.
  • • Applied and Library views — Templates and Frames now open on Applied to this event, with Library for browsing everything. Previously these always listed the whole shared library, which made a new event look like it had inherited the last one's design.
  • • Reload returns to Home — Closes the open event rather than restoring the last screen.

Security

  • • Event storage cleanup locked down — The routine that deletes an event's photos and gallery records ran with elevated privileges, never checked the caller, and was reachable by any signed-in account, so one operator could delete another operator's event photos. It now verifies ownership and restricts access. This fix is applied on our side; no action is needed from operators.

Fixed

  • • Fixed deleted events reappearing after a restart.
  • • Fixed new events opening with the previous event's branding and settings.
  • • Fixed saving settings overwriting every event. Booth-level settings still apply to all events; event-level settings now apply only to the event you have open.
  • • Fixed the template editor discarding in-progress work and carrying frames between templates.
  • • Fixed booths reporting 0.3.0 regardless of build.
  • • Fixed the "Account services are not configured" error on every event delete.

v0.3.0

Template Editor, Visual Tones & Guided Tour

August 29, 2026

The template editor gets an icon toolbar, sliders, dark mode and fullscreen editing; tone selection now previews real photos from the session instead of abstract swatches; and a first-run guided tour walks new operators through the app. Booth health is also monitored around the clock.

New

  • • Template Editor overhaul — The editor toolbar is now icon-based for a cleaner layout. Slider controls replace raw number inputs for opacity, border radius, and similar properties. Dark mode is fully supported throughout the editor. New slot actions include Clone (duplicate a slot in one click) and Toggle Fit (switch between fill and fit modes). Fullscreen editing mode is available for large-screen setups. Aspect ratio is now locked by default to prevent accidental distortion.
  • • Visual tone preview — The tone selection screen now shows real photo samples from your session instead of abstract swatches. A horizontal filter strip replaces the raw number display, making tones easier to compare at a glance. The preset library has been expanded with new styles.
  • • Guided onboarding tour — First-run tour walks new operators through Home, Events, the Template Editor, Settings, Reports, and Help Center. An interactive spotlight highlights each area as you go. The Create Event step is gated — you must create a real event before the tour advances. A "Take a tour" button in Settings replays the tour at any time.
  • • Health monitor — 24/7 booth health monitoring with background checks for disk space, memory usage, session folder count, and app uptime. Alerts surface in the dashboard before a problem affects guests.

Fixed

  • • Fixed template workspace canvas rendering incorrectly after rapid slot edits.
  • • Fixed dark mode flash on initial load caused by a theme race condition.
  • • Fixed frames disappearing when switching between events quickly.
  • • Fixed portrait canvas not centering correctly in the editor.
  • • Fixed gallery upload using the service-role key instead of the operator's own JWT — uploads now authenticate with the operator's session.

Discount codes join PayMongo checkout, Gallery QR and Gallery Branding no longer require admin access, and the Windows installer build is more reliable — plus the embedded licensing server has been removed from the app entirely in favor of Supabase.

  • • Fixed installer build attempting to recompile better-sqlite3 native module without Python — native module rebuild is skipped since it is no longer used in the distributed app.
  • • Fixed "Photuna account services are not configured on this build" error when opening QR Gallery for events.

v0.2.5

Gallery QR Workflows, Sample Filters & Reliability Overhaul

August 1, 2026

Gallery QR workflows, template library improvements, profile management fixes, and a significant reliability overhaul for post-login data loading.

New

  • • Pre-session Event Gallery QR — Admins can generate an Event Gallery QR code for any event before a single photo is taken. Open the Gallery QR modal and click Generate QR — the link is created instantly, persisted for 90 days, and can be shared with clients right away so they're ready to scan the moment the booth starts shooting.
  • • Gallery QR button per event in Events Library — Every event card now has a QR Gallery action button, active only when the account has an active Gallery add-on. It opens a modal showing both the pre-session event QR and all individual session QR codes, with copy-link and Download QR options.
  • • Download QR as PNG — Both event-level and session-level QR codes can now be downloaded as a high-resolution 512×512 PNG directly from the Gallery QR modal — no screenshotting required.
  • • Format filter chips in Samples — Templates — The Samples panel now has filter chips (All · 4×6 · 2×6 · 6×4 · 6×2) so operators can quickly find templates matching their current paper format without scrolling through unrelated layouts.
  • • Format filter chips in Samples — Frames — The same filter chips are available for frames in the Samples panel. Each card's preview image automatically switches to the selected format's variant, so you always see an accurate representation of the frame before applying it.

Improved

  • • Events load instantly after login — no Ctrl+R required — The events list now reads from the local store immediately, before the Supabase sync network call. Previously the app waited for the full cloud round-trip before showing any data, so a slow or unsettled auth token caused an empty events list until you manually refreshed. Events now appear as soon as the screen loads.
  • • Auto-refresh on window focus and periodic license sync — The app now silently refreshes your license status when the window regains focus (throttled to once every 30 seconds) and on a 5-minute interval while running. Events are also re-read on focus so multi-device or web-app changes appear without restarting.
  • • Template limit enforced in Samples section — Applying or adding a sample template now respects your account's template limit, with a toast notification when the limit is reached — matching the behavior already in place for the main templates library.
  • • Profile photo picker reliability — The "Update photo" button in Account → Profile is more reliable and now shows a loading state on the button while the upload is in progress.
  • • Manual reload button for Events Library — A refresh icon in the Events Library header lets you manually re-read events from the local store without a full page reload — useful when events were created from another device and the focus-based sync hasn't fired yet.
  • • Faster settings sync on first login — Eliminated a redundant network round-trip when reading your settings, and removed a race condition where data could fail to load silently on first login.
  • • More reliable avatar uploads — Profile photo uploads are now packaged in a way that's fully compatible with the desktop app's upload pipeline, fixing a rare "no image data received" error some operators saw.

Fixed

  • • Template limit not enforced when limit was zero — A logic bug meant an entitlement of exactly 0 templates was treated as unlimited instead of blocked. Template limits are now enforced correctly at every tier.
  • • Free plan users limited to 1 template instead of 3 — Free-tier accounts were capped at 1 template due to a hardcoded default; corrected to the intended 3-template free plan limit.
  • • Saving profile wiped existing avatar URL — Saving your profile while the photo field was empty (e.g. after a slow page load) could accidentally erase your previously uploaded avatar. Your avatar is now preserved unless you actually upload a new one.
  • • Uploaded avatar not refreshing in browser due to caching — After uploading a new photo, the old one sometimes kept showing because the browser served a cached copy. New uploads now always display immediately.
  • • Cloud sync could clobber local data with empty values — A merge-strategy bug meant syncing with the cloud could occasionally overwrite locally-saved settings, appearance, and template data with blank values. Local data now always takes priority for anything not already present in the cloud.
  • • Events, templates, frames, and palettes occasionally failing to load — A storage bug could cause these lists to come back in the wrong data shape when empty, silently breaking the app's ability to merge and display them. Fixed so empty lists always load correctly.
v0.2.4

Payment Providers, Trial Onboarding & Template Overhaul

July 28, 2026

New

  • • Free trial onboarding modal — Logged-in users who haven't yet activated their free trial see a one-time pop-up explaining what the 14-day trial includes. Skipped for users with an active subscription, a redeemed trial, or an expired trial.
  • • Multi-payment provider support — Operators can now configure one of four payment providers in Business settings: PayMongo (Philippines), Stripe (global), Xendit (Indonesia & Philippines), or PayPal (200+ countries). Only one provider can be active at a time — switching is instant.
  • – Stripe — Accepts card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, SEPA Direct Debit, and iDEAL.
  • – Xendit — Accepts card, OVO, DANA, GoPay, LinkAja, ShopeePay, QRIS, and five virtual account banks (BCA, BNI, BRI, Mandiri) plus Alfamart and Indomaret convenience stores.
  • – PayPal — Accepts PayPal wallet, Pay Later (BNPL), Venmo, and card.
  • • Dual-strip print mode — Templates now carry a Print Mode field: Single (one print per sheet) or 2-Strip (two strips side-by-side on one sheet, cut down the middle). Selectable from the template editor for 4×6 and 6×4 layouts, with a badge on each template card.
  • • 6×2 and 6×4 landscape layouts — Full support for landscape orientations: 6×2 landscape strip and 6×4 landscape print, alongside the existing 2×6 portrait strip and 4×6 portrait print.
  • • Refreshed default template library — 18 curated templates across six formats (2×6, 6×2, 4×6, 6×4, 4×6 Dual Strip, 6×4 Dual Strip), three per format.
  • • Redesigned default frames — Six production-ready overlays pre-rendered at all four layout sizes: Studio White, Noir & Gold, Rose Gold, Floral Corner, Film Classic, and Confetti.
  • • Automatic update announcements — Photuna checks for updates on launch and every four hours. A dismissible banner with a direct "Update now" link appears when a new version is available.
  • • Sample Layouts gallery — Browse all built-in templates and frames from a new Samples tab before committing to them for an event.
  • • DNP & HiTi auto-cut detection — Settings → Printing scans the Windows print queue for connected DNP/HiTi printers and reports whether 2×6 strip cut is configured, with brand-specific setup instructions.

Improved

  • • Tap to Start button sizing — Fixed a rendering bug where the button appeared far smaller than intended on kiosk displays; now uses viewport-relative clamp() sizing.
  • • Start Button editor — Label, font, background colour, hover colour, and text colour are now grouped in a single "Start Button" card inside Branding settings.
  • • Template card badges — Every card now shows Single, 2-Strip, or Strip so operators can identify print mode at a glance.
  • • Frame previews for all layouts — Overlays render correctly at all four layout sizes regardless of which layout is active.
  • • Print mode selector in template editor — Switching a 4×6 or 6×4 template to 2-Strip shows a contextual note describing the cut-line and slot requirements.
  • • Blue colour theme — The admin dashboard and auth screens now use a clean blue accent palette throughout, replacing the previous teal theme.
  • • Default badges — Template and frame cards show a "Default" badge for built-in items.
  • • Responsive photobooth screens — All kiosk-facing screens scale correctly across a wider range of display sizes using fluid clamp() values.
  • • Cleaner print preview — Removed the redundant "Your photo is ready!" label so the layout no longer crowds the progress animation.
  • • 4×6 and 2×6 focus — Removed the 4×4 square layout; Photuna now focuses on the two formats real dye-sublimation printers support.

Fixed

  • • Trial modal false positives — The onboarding prompt was incorrectly appearing for accounts with active subscriptions, redeemed trials, and trial-unavailable status. Root cause was a race condition where the license object was evaluated before it had fully loaded; all edge cases are now correctly gated.
